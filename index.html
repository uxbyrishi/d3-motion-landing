How do I open D3 Motion on macOS? (Gatekeeper Instructions)

Because D3 Motion is an independent professional utility distributed directly outside the Mac App Store, macOS Gatekeeper may show a security notice on first launch: "D3 Motion cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer."

The Standard Way to Open (First Launch Only):
  1. Drag D3 Motion.app into your /Applications folder.
  2. In Applications, Right-click (or hold Control and click) on D3 Motion.
  3. Click Open in the context menu.
  4. When the macOS confirmation box appears, click the Open button.
✓ You only have to do this once. macOS permanently remembers your approval, and you can double-click to launch it anytime afterward!
Missed that and got blocked with only "Cancel" or "Move to Trash"?
Option 1 (System Settings): Open Apple Menu  > System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section where you will see: "D3 Motion was blocked from use...". Click the Open Anyway button, enter your Mac password, and click Open.

Option 2 (Instant One-Line Terminal Command): Open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter) and paste:
xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/D3 Motion.app"
Press Return, and the application will open immediately without any security prompts!
